# Credits

The tutoring method comes from [Jane Street](https://github.com/janestreet)'s [`socratic-tutor`](https://github.com/janestreet/agent_skills/tree/7c6715c29548a3b39d4a9545c698ee4fd17a7213/socratic-tutor) skill. These parts of `SKILL.md` are reproduced from it, mostly word for word: the intent guard, the diagnostic, the 0-5 skill map and its rendering, the teaching loop, the evaluation rules, the mastery target and final assessment, and the anytime commands. This skill aims that method at a course's units and lessons. What's new: the sources table, ending each lesson on its exercise, site adapters with `SETUP.md`, `scripts/replica.py`, and self-repair when a site changes. `socratic-tutor` is copied in rather than depended on, so this skill installs standalone; this file carries the attribution, and the MIT notice that license requires.
